# ASHA 2026: "Stay Connected" Google Form setup

**About 10 minutes to set up.** One form works for both sign options. Its description holds the two app links, so on the single-QR sign people can try an app *or* fill out the form from the same page.

## 1. Create the form
1. Go to **forms.google.com** while signed in to your **SpeechLP** Google account, and click **Blank form**.
2. **Title:** `Great meeting you at ASHA!`
3. **Description:** paste this exactly. Google Forms turns the links into clickable links.

```
Thanks for stopping by Booth 2046!

▶ Try SpeechLP (speech sound practice kids do out loud):
https://app.speechlp.com/?utm_source=asha2026&utm_medium=form&utm_campaign=booth2046

▶ Try SpeechTherapist.app (SLP documentation + assign SpeechLP for home practice):
https://speechtherapist.app/?utm_source=asha2026&utm_medium=form&utm_campaign=booth2046

Or stay connected below. It takes about 30 seconds.
```

## 2. Add the questions

| # | Question | Type | Required | Options |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Name | Short answer | Yes | |
| 2 | Email | Short answer, with **Response validation: Text → Email address** | Yes | |
| 3 | Which best describes you? | Multiple choice | Yes | SLP / SLPA · Student · Practice owner / administrator · Researcher / educator · Other |
| 4 | What are you interested in? | Checkboxes (people can pick more than one) | No | SpeechLP · SpeechTherapist.app · Research / collaboration · Just staying connected |
| 5 | Email updates | Checkboxes | No | `I'd like to receive product updates and occasional emails from SpeechLP.` |

Question 4 drops the "Both" option from the mockup. With checkboxes, people just tick both apps.

**Keep question 5 separate and optional.** Only people who tick it get added to marketing emails. That's the consent record for your Klaviyo follow-up.

## 3. Settings (important)
Click **Settings** (the gear icon or the Settings tab):
- **Responses → Collect email addresses:** **Do not collect.** You already ask for email, and turning this on can make people sign in.
- **Responses → Limit to 1 response:** **OFF.** If it's on, visitors must sign in with Google, and most will quit.
- **Presentation → Confirmation message:**
  `Thank you! We'll be in touch after ASHA. Come say hi at Booth 2046 anytime.`

## 4. Save responses to a Sheet
**Responses** tab → **Link to Sheets** → **Create a new spreadsheet** → name it `ASHA 2026 Stay Connected`. Share it with Alissa.

## 5. Get the link and send it to me
1. Click **Send** → the **link icon** 🔗 → tick **Shorten URL** → **Copy**.
2. Paste the link here. I'll put it into both signs and make the final print PDFs.

## 6. Test it before you print
- Open the link on your phone **in a private/incognito window**. It should open without asking you to sign in.
- Submit one test response, check that it shows up in the Sheet, then delete the test row.

## After ASHA
Export the Sheet and import the people who ticked question 5 into Klaviyo as **subscribed** (they gave consent). Everyone else gets only a personal follow-up, not marketing emails. I can do this import for you.
