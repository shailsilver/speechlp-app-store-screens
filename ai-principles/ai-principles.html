SpeechLP & SpeechTherapist.app

How we use AI

AI helps us save SLPs time and give kids more practice. It doesn't replace clinical judgment, and it never will.

A note from our co-CEOs

We're both practicing speech-language pathologists. We built SpeechLP and SpeechTherapist.app because we wanted better tools for our own caseloads. These are the commitments we hold ourselves to.

  1. The SLP makes the clinical decisions.

    AI drafts SOAP notes, reports, parent updates and lesson plans from your session. Nothing is final until a licensed clinician reviews, edits and signs it. You are always the author of the record.

  2. Your data is labeled honestly.

    You choose whether each target is counted, estimated or left unscored, and your notes and goal charts show which is which. AI never turns an estimate into a count.

  3. Children's voices stay on their device.

    In SpeechLP, a child's speech is processed on the phone, tablet or computer, and the audio is never stored. We keep only the text of what the app heard, so families and SLPs can see progress. [How SpeechTherapist.app live transcription handles session audio.]

  4. We don't sell data or use it for ads.

    We never sell personal information or use it for advertising. We don't use identifiable client or child data to train AI models without explicit consent. Our AI providers are bound by business associate agreements and don't keep or train on your data.

  5. We're honest about what our tools can and can't do.

    Our screener is a useful first signal, not a diagnosis. SpeechLP's speech scoring is automated, and it hasn't yet been validated against SLP ratings. We're working with university researchers to test it, and we'll share what we find, whatever it shows.

  6. Privacy laws are our floor, not our ceiling.

    We comply with HIPAA, COPPA, FERPA and PIPEDA. A business associate agreement is available for clinicians and organizations. [Link to our list of subprocessors.]

  7. Families and SLPs stay in control.

    Parents choose whether to connect with their child's SLP and can disconnect at any time. Families and clinicians can ask us to delete their data. [Deletion timeline.]

  8. Clinicians stay accountable, and we help.

    We design our tools so SLPs can meet their professional and ethical responsibilities, including the ASHA Code of Ethics. If an AI draft gets something wrong, tell us and we'll fix it.

  9. We'll keep this page current.

    As AI and the rules around it change, we'll update these principles and note the date.
