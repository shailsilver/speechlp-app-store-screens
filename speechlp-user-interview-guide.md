# SpeechLP User Feedback Call – Interview Guide

Goal: learn, don't sell. ~30 minutes. She should be talking 80% of the time.

## Before you start

- Be brutally honest: "You can't offend me. The harsh stuff is what actually helps us."
- Open-ended, not yes/no. If you slip, follow with "why?" or "tell me more."
- Ask what she *did*, not what she *would do*. Get stories about specific moments.
- Shut up. Don't fill pauses. Don't defend or explain the app. Park feature ideas until the end.
- Drill down on anything interesting. Parrot back the important stuff: "So what I'm hearing is…"

## The 10 questions

Her life and behavior first. Product feedback second. Don't mix them.

**Why she started**
1. Take me back to when you first went looking for something like SpeechLP. What was going on?
2. What had you tried before that? How did it go?

**What actually happens**
3. Walk me through the last time you used the app, step by step.
4. How has usage changed since you started? (If it dropped: what was going on then?)

**Results**
5. Have you noticed anything different about your child's speech? Give me a specific example.
6. What hasn't changed that you were hoping would?

**Issues** (the important part)
7. What's been the most frustrating part of using it?
8. Tell me about a time it didn't do what you expected, or you got stuck.

**Introductions**
9. Do you know other parents who'd get something out of this, or who are already using it? I'd love to chat with them the same way. Would you introduce me?

**Close**
10. What should I have asked that I didn't?

Then confirm her email for the $25 Amazon card.

## Follow-ups (use these instead of new questions)

"Tell me more." · "Why?" · "What happened next?" · "Can you give me an example?" · "What did you do about it?" · silence

## Backup probes (only if a thread runs dry)

- How did you find SpeechLP? What were you searching for?
- Who drives the sessions, you or your child? What's their reaction when it's time?
- Has a therapist, teacher, or family member commented on their speech recently?
- What do you attribute any change to? (Let her say app, therapy, age, or school.)
- Are there parts you skip or your child loses interest in? Why?
- If the app disappeared tomorrow, what would you do instead?
- (Last, kept separate) What tipped you into paying? / What would have to be true for you to pay?
- Have you mentioned it to anyone? What did you say, and what did they say?
- Is there a parent group where speech concerns come up a lot?

## After the call (within the hour)

Brain-dump into a shared doc: trigger, real usage, observed results, top 3 issues, direct quotes, intros promised, anything that surprised you. Send the gift card and intro follow-ups the same day. Look for patterns across calls, not conclusions from one.
