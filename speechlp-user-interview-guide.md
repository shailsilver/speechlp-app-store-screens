# SpeechLP User Feedback Call – Interview Guide

Goal: learn, don't sell. Understand why she started, what's actually happening in her home with the app, what's broken, and who else we should talk to.

---

## Reminders (read before the call)

Based on Giff Constable's "12 Tips for Early Customer Development Interviews":

- **Open the call by disarming politeness.** "Thank you so much. The most useful thing you can do for me is be brutally honest. Nothing you say will offend me. The harsh stuff is actually the most valuable."
- **Open-ended, not yes/no.** "Tell me about…", "Walk me through…", "What happened when…" instead of "Do you like X?"
- **Past behavior, not hypotheticals.** Ask what she *did*, not what she *would do*. "Would you use…" and "would you pay…" answers are unreliable.
- **Listen. Don't talk.** Aim for her talking 80%+. Silence is fine; let her fill it.
- **Don't sell, don't defend, don't explain.** If she criticizes something, say "tell me more" instead of justifying it.
- **Don't lead.** Avoid "Did you find the exercises helpful?" Ask "How did the exercises go?"
- **Follow your nose and drill down.** When something interesting comes up, ask "why?" a few times. Go off-script.
- **Parrot back to confirm.** "So what I'm hearing is… did I get that right?"
- **Get psyched to hear bad news.** The point of the call is to find out what's wrong.
- **Take notes (or record with permission) and write up within an hour.**
- **End with asking for introductions.** Every time.
- Keep it to ~30 minutes. Prioritize sections 2, 4, and 6 if short on time.

---

## 1. Warm-up / context (3 min)

- Tell me a little about your child. How old are they, and how would you describe their speech right now?
- Walk me through a typical day. Where does speech practice (of any kind) fit in, if at all?
- Who else is involved in your child's speech development? (therapist, school, partner, grandparents)

## 2. Why she started (5 min) – the trigger

- Take me back to when you first went looking for something like SpeechLP. What was going on?
- What was the moment you decided "I need to do something about this"?
- What had you tried before? (SLP, school services, YouTube, other apps, books, nothing) How did those go?
- How did you find SpeechLP? What were you searching for or where did you see it?
- What was going through your head when you first downloaded it? What did you expect it to do?
- What, if anything, almost stopped you from signing up?

## 3. Actual usage (5 min) – behavior, not opinions

- Walk me through the last time you used the app. What did you do, step by step?
- How often has it actually been used in the past two weeks? (Not "how often do you intend to")
- Who's driving the sessions, you or your child? Where are you, what time of day?
- What does your child do when it's time to use it? What's their reaction?
- Has usage changed since you started? If it dropped off, what happened around that time?
- What do you do with the app in a week where things are busy or chaotic?

## 4. Results / differences in child's speech (5 min)

- Have you noticed anything different about your child's speech since you started? Tell me about it.
- Can you give me a specific example, a word or sound, where you've seen a change?
- What do you attribute that to? (Could be the app, therapy, age, school. Let her say it.)
- Has anyone else, a therapist, teacher, or family member, commented on their speech recently?
- What has *not* changed that you were hoping would?
- How do you know whether it's working? What are you watching for?

## 5. Issues and frustrations (7 min) – the most important section

- What's been the most frustrating or annoying part of using the app?
- Tell me about a time the app didn't do what you expected.
- Has anything felt confusing? Where did you get stuck?
- What have you tried to do that you couldn't figure out how to do?
- What does your child dislike or lose interest in?
- Are there parts you just skip or ignore? Why?
- Has anything made you think "this isn't right for us" or consider stopping?
- If you could change one thing, what would it be? Why that one?
- (If she's hesitant) "What's the thing you'd tell a friend about this app but wouldn't normally tell me?"

## 6. Value, alternatives, and the money question (3 min)

- How would you describe what SpeechLP does to another parent?
- If the app disappeared tomorrow, what would you do instead?
- What are you comparing it to in your head when you think about whether it's worth it? (Be ready to hear "nothing" or "a therapist.")
- (If she pays) Tell me about the moment you decided to pay. What tipped it?
- (If she doesn't) What would have to be true for you to pay for something like this?

## 7. Introductions (2 min) – always ask

- Do you know any other parents who are using SpeechLP?
- Have you mentioned it to anyone? What did you say? What did they say?
- Is there anyone you think would get something out of it? Why them?
- "I'd love to chat with them the same way we're chatting now. Would you be willing to introduce me, or share my contact info with them?"
- Is there a parent group, Facebook group, or community where speech concerns come up a lot?
- Did your child's SLP or teacher know about the app? What did they say?

## 8. Close (1 min)

- Is there anything I should have asked but didn't?
- Can I follow up with you if we build something based on this?
- Confirm email for the $25 Amazon card and thank her.

---

## Follow-up prompts (use throughout)

- "Tell me more about that."
- "Why?" / "Why was that important?"
- "What happened next?"
- "Can you give me a specific example?"
- "How did that make you feel?"
- "What did you do about it?"
- (Silence. Count to five.)

## After the call (within 1 hour)

- Write up notes: trigger, usage reality, observed results, top 3 issues, quotes, intros promised.
- Note any surprises or anything that contradicted what we assumed.
- Send the gift card and any intro-request follow-up the same day.
