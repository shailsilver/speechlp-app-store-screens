# App Store review request emails

Two versions: one for **families** and one for **SLPs**. Send them this week, before ASHA, so new reviews are on the listing when booth visitors look you up.

**Review link:** `https://apps.apple.com/app/id[APP_ID]?action=write-review`
Find your App ID in App Store Connect → App Information (it's a number, like 1234567890). This link opens the review form directly.

## Rules to follow (Apple's)
- **No rewards.** Don't offer anything (a free month, posters, entries) in exchange for a review. Apple prohibits incentivized reviews.
- **Don't ask for 5 stars.** Ask for an honest review.
- **Inside the app, use only Apple's built-in review prompt.** It shows at most 3 times a year per person. Email is fine for asking directly.

## Who to send to
- **Families:** practiced in the last 30 days, with at least 3 sessions (from PostHog). Engaged families are the most likely to review, and their experience is current.
- **SLPs:** connected at least one family through SpeechTherapist.app, or told you they've recommended SpeechLP.

## Email A — Families

**Subject:** A quick favor from two SLPs
**Preview:** It takes 30 seconds and helps other families find us

Hi [First name],

We're Alissa and Mari, the speech-language pathologists behind SpeechLP, and a small team built it with us.

If SpeechLP has helped [child's name / your child] practice at home, would you leave a quick review on the App Store? Other parents read reviews when they're deciding how to help their child, and a few honest sentences go a long way.

**→ [Leave a review](https://apps.apple.com/app/id[APP_ID]?action=write-review)**

Some ideas if you're not sure what to say: which game your child likes, a sound they've been working on, or how practice at home has felt.

And if something isn't working well, please just reply to this email. We read every message and would love to fix it.

Thank you for practicing with us,
Alissa Fox, M.S., CCC-SLP & Mari Gottlieb, M.A., CCC-SLP
Co-founders, SpeechLP

## After reviews come in
- **Reply to every review in App Store Connect, especially critical ones.** Keep it short, kind and specific: "Thanks for telling us. We've fixed the microphone issue in version X." A thoughtful reply often reassures parents more than another 5-star review.
- **Watch for patterns.** If several reviews mention the same issue (for example, noisy rooms or a hard-to-recognize sound), add it to the roadmap.
- **Save the best quotes** (with permission) for the website and the Founding SLP email.
