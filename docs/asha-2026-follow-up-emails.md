## How it fits together

1. **At the booth:** people scan one QR code (on a small stand sign) that opens a 30-second form. The form decides which version of Email 1 they get. Tie it to the giveaways:
   - **Poster** (speech sound ages): free for anyone who fills out the form. "Scan for your free poster."
   - **Book** (*Learn to Speak and Read*): free when an SLP starts a SpeechTherapist.app trial at the booth. The trial signup collects their email, so they don't need the form too. Tag them "Started trial at ASHA" so they get Version A or B of Email 1 and the trial reminder in Email 3.
2. **After ASHA:** four emails over about two and a half weeks. They skip Thanksgiving week (Thanksgiving is Thu Nov 26).
3. **Main call to action:** book a 15-minute Zoom walkthrough. You don't have to do long demos at a busy booth with two people.

### Booth form (Google Forms, Typeform, Tally, or your CRM)

Keep it to 6 required questions so it takes under 30 seconds.

1. Name *
2. Email *
3. I am a… * — School SLP · Private practice SLP / owner · Clinic or hospital SLP · Clinical fellow · Grad student · University faculty · Other
4. State / country *
5. I'm most interested in… * — SpeechTherapist.app (my documentation) · SpeechLP (home practice for my families) · Both
6. Languages your clients need besides English (optional) — Spanish · Other: ____ · English only
7. ☐ I'd like to be considered for the Founding SLP group (feedback in exchange for perks)
8. ☐ (Faculty) I'm interested in a research partnership
9. One sentence on what caught your eye (optional) ☐ OK to quote me with first name + role

**If the wifi is bad:** phones use cellular, so the QR code usually still works. Bring a paper clipboard with the same questions as a backup, and type it up each night.
