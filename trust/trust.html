Trust & privacy

Children, families and clinicians trust us with sensitive information. Here's exactly how we protect it, in plain language.

Children's voices are never stored

When a child practices in SpeechLP, their speech is processed on the device and the audio is never saved. We keep only the text of what the app heard, so families and SLPs can see progress.

What we collect, and why

  • Practice results: which words were practiced, what the app heard, and whether each try was accepted. This powers progress tracking.
  • Account details: a parent's or clinician's name and email, and the child's first name or nickname and age. [confirm exact fields]
  • Clinical records (SpeechTherapist.app): the session data, notes and goals that clinicians enter or approve.
  • App usage: anonymous analytics to fix bugs and improve the games. [confirm PostHog settings for child profiles]

What we never do

  • We never sell personal information.
  • We never show ads or use data for advertising.
  • We never share a child's practice data with an SLP unless the family connects to that SLP.

Who can see a child's data

  • Parents and caregivers on the family account.
  • The child's SLP, only after the family accepts a connection code or invite. Families can disconnect at any time.
  • Our team, only when needed to provide support or keep the service running. [confirm access policy]

How we keep it secure

  • Data is encrypted in transit and at rest. [confirm]
  • Data is hosted in [region / provider].
  • Access to production data is limited and logged. [confirm]
  • We work only with vendors who meet our privacy standards. [link to subprocessor list]

Research, done carefully

We study aggregated practice data to learn what helps children progress, and we publish what we find, limitations included. Research data is de-identified, small groups are suppressed so no child can be identified, and account details never leave SpeechLP. University partners work under a data use agreement and their own ethics approval.
